## 1. INTRODUCTION

This document presents the semester project developed under a Project-Based Learning (PBL) methodology. The project brings together contributions from four supporting curriculum units: Generative AI, Applied Deep Learning, Emerging Technologies, and Interface Design.

### Expected Learning Outcomes
By the end of the semester, students are expected to:
- Develop a working Emergent Technology product that meaningfully integrates interaction design, concept development, and asset production (e.g., text images, sound, or video), as well as visual reflection on authority, ethics, and limitations.
- Appreciate how the project supports understanding and practical application of generative models (e.g., CNN-based approaches, GANs, VAEs), differentiation layers for creating, transforming, or driving content and behaviours within the experience.
- Explore VR/AR ecosystems and sensor hardware, including body/hand tracking, spatial input, and relevant software/frameworks, enabling integration of modalities such as hand tracking, eye tracking, or Haptic feedback where appropriate.

## 3. PROJECT SCOPE & THEMES

### Context
The results will be integrated into the fashion event calendar organized by the Master's in Branding and Fashion Design, who will be responsible for the event's production and curation.

### Project Scope
Students from M-CCIA are expected to develop an exploratory and speculative project, suitable for public presentation as an interactive exhibition experience. The theme and output format are open, but must relate to the fashion universe in some way.

### Potential Topics
- **Identity**: In what ways does our expression of identity evolve with technology?
- **Body**: How does technology become a body extension, changing the way the body is perceived and presented?
- **Society**: How will technology impact how we build community, relate to one another, and communicate through what we wear?
- **Culture**: How will new technologies impact communication of cultural concepts and messages?
- **Wearables**: In what ways will technology be used functionally and/or communicatively on the body?
- **Industry 5.0**: How will humans and intelligent machines collaborate in creative and productive processes?
- **Consumption**: How will technology alter the shopping experience for fashion products?
