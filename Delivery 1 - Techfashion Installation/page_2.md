# CORE QUESTION
How does algorithmic mediation reshape authorship in fashion?

## CONCEPT OVERVIEW
The Oracle That Wears Us is an interactive fashion installation. A robotic drawing plotter creates wearable fabric pieces based on audience interaction. Visitors interact with an arcade-style "Oracle" machine. Their movements and inputs are translated into unique generative patterns. These patterns are drawn directly onto fabric. Throughout the event, the fabric pieces accumulate into a collective garment.

Authorship shifts from designer → machine → public.
